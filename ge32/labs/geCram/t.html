这是产品简介页面。功能聚焦在个人安全与合法证据保存，而非侵犯隐私或非法拍摄。

版本：Beta • 目标：个人安全记录与合规证据保全

产品概述

功能很简单：在不违反当地法律与他人隐私权的前提下，提供一个便于保存关键时刻的工具。典型场景包括：发生争执需要记录证据、记录突发事故的现场情况、或用于个人安全备份。

主要功能

秘密拍摄
在不显示拍摄界面的情况下，进行秘密拍摄。
隐私保护
本工具数据保存当前使用的浏览器，而非后台服务器，但需要清楚，一旦浏览器被卸载或者数据清空，那么内容将永久消失。
证据导出
支持将视频下载，便于提交给执法与法律机构（仅在合法情形下）。
位置与时间戳
自动保存时间戳与必要的元数据，帮助证明记录的时空信息。

合规与责任声明

重要：使用本工具前务必了解并遵守所在地法律法规。严禁用于跟踪、偷拍或任何侵犯他人隐私的行为。用户须自行承担因违法使用导致的一切责任。我们鼓励在可行的情况下先征得对方同意或在公共场合合法记录。

Press Release — For Immediate Release

ISeeU Announces New Personal Safety Recorder Focused on Lawful Evidence Preservation

October 20, 2025 — ISeeU, a small team of privacy-minded developers, today unveiled a beta version of its Personal Safety Recorder, a mobile-first tool designed to help individuals legally document incidents while prioritizing consent and privacy.

The recorder provides quick access to audio and video capture, secure export options for legal professionals, and clear guidance on lawful usage. “Our goal is to empower people to protect themselves and preserve factual records when necessary — not to enable covert surveillance,” said Li Wei, project lead at ISeeU. “Transparency and respect for privacy are baked into the product.”

Pilot users praised the app's straightforward workflow and its emphasis on secure handling of recordings. One early adopter said, “I used ISeeU after a delivery dispute; having a clear, time-stamped record resolved the issue quickly and professionally.”

ISeeU also partners with local legal advisors to provide up-to-date information about recording laws across different jurisdictions. The company recommends users familiarize themselves with local regulations and, whenever possible, obtain consent prior to recording.

About ISeeU
ISeeU is a developer initiative focused on tools that balance personal safety with ethical design. The team is committed to legal compliance, privacy-first architecture, and transparency.
